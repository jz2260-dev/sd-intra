# Temporary Account Decommission SOP

---

## Quick Start (Use This First)

Follow this checklist when reviewing temporary accounts:

- [ ] Identify temporary account (Student Worker / TF / GF / Visiting Scholar)  
- [ ] Locate original creation request (ServiceNow – old or new)  
- [ ] Confirm sponsor and proposed end date  
- [ ] Contact sponsor for confirmation  
- [ ] Process response:
  - Extension → Forward to Accounts team  
  - Deprovision → Notify Accounts team  
- [ ] Update Asana record and comments  
- [ ] Document actions in incident/request  

If anything is unclear, stop and escalate to Endpoint Management.

---

## Step-by-Step (Use if You Need More Detail)

### 1. Identify Account

Applies to:

- Student Workers  
- Teaching & Graduate Fellows (TF/GF)  
- Visiting Scholars  

---

### 2. Locate Original Request

- Look up the account in:
  - ServiceNow (new system)  
  - ServiceNow (old system, if applicable)  

- Copy into incident/request:
  - Sponsor  
  - Proposed End Date  

If old system access is needed:
- Contact Johnny  

Alternative:
- Check HATS notes section for sponsor information  

---

### 3. Contact Sponsor

Send email to sponsor or last known sponsor:

Hi [Sponsor],

As part of our annual account review process, could you please confirm whether this account is still needed, since you are listed as the sponsor?

Name: [Name of account]  
NetID: [NetID of account]  
End date: [Proposed End Date]  
Account notes: [Role and Department, if known]  

If this work account is still required, please provide a new end date within the next year.

If this work account is no longer required, we will proceed with deprovisioning and removal of work access after the end date. Please make sure to transfer any files or data you need from this account before deprovisioning begins.

-  
Signature  

---

### 4. Handle Sponsor Response

#### A. Extension Requested

- Forward email to: accounts@stern.nyu.edu  
- Include requested duration and sponsor response  

**Rule:**  
- Extensions are limited to maximum 1 year per request  

#### Update Records

- Update Proposed End Date in Asana  
- Copy sponsor response into Asana comments  

#### Notify Sponsor

Hi [Sponsor],

Thank you for the update.

I've forwarded your request over to our Accounts team and we will reach back out to you as the new expiration date is approaching.

-  
Signature  

---

#### B. Deprovisioning Confirmed

- Assign task to Kirk  
- Update Asana comments with sponsor confirmation  

#### Notify Sponsor

Hi [Sponsor],

Thank you for getting back to me.

I have forwarded this information over to our Accounts team and will begin the deprovisioning process.

-  
Signature  

---

### 5. Update Records

Update:

- Asana:
  - Status  
  - Comments  
  - New end date OR deprovision confirmation  

- incident/request:
  - Sponsor response  
  - Action taken (extended / deprovision requested)  

---

## Escalation

If issues cannot be resolved:

- Escalate to Endpoint Management  
- Include account details, sponsor, actions taken, and errors  

---

## Something Went Wrong (Quick Fixes)

| Issue | What to Check |
|------|---------------|
| Sponsor not found | Check ServiceNow / HATS / escalate |
| No response from sponsor | Follow up or escalate |
| Extension unclear | Clarify duration (max 1 year) |
| Conflicting info | Confirm with department |
| Ownership unclear | Check original request |

---

## Done When

- Sponsor has responded  
- Account is either extended (via Accounts team) OR submitted for deprovisioning  
- Asana is updated  
- incident/request is documented  

---

## Notes for Technicians

- Service Desk does not directly decommission accounts  
- Always confirm sponsor before action  
- Never assume continued usage without confirmation  
- Extensions must follow 1-year maximum rule  
- Ensure all updates are reflected in Asana and incident/request  

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

Service Desk:
- Perform account review  
- Contact sponsor  
- Coordinate extension or deprovisioning  
- Update Asana and incident/request  

Endpoint Management:
- Support escalations and complex cases  

Accounts Team:
- Execute account deprovisioning  
- Process and approve extensions  
- Manage account lifecycle
