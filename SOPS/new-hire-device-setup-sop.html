SOP - Internal Procedure

New Hire Device Setup

Use this SOP to validate the request, assign the right hardware, complete imaging or enrollment, and prepare a new hire device for a clean handoff.

SOP Flow

Validate the new hire request, match the person to the correct device path, confirm access and apps, then close with reporting and handoff readiness.

Overview

Use this SOP for onboarding a new hire with a prepared Windows or Apple device.

Primary outcomeThe new hire receives a fully configured device with correct access, applications, and asset ownership.
Use whenA request is for a new employee or equivalent onboarding setup.
Do not use whenThe work is only a repair, replacement without onboarding context, or a temporary access-only request.

Prerequisites

Confirm these before preparing the device.

Request confirms a new hire setup and includes start date.

User full name, NetID, and department are available.

Device type or platform expectation is known.

Device inventory or assignment path is confirmed.

Decision Points

Use these checks before configuring the device.

Is the platform Windows or Apple?That determines whether the work routes into MDT or Jamf imaging.
Is a device already assigned?If not, coordinate assignment before starting setup work.
Does the user need temporary access?If yes, use the temporary account provisioning SOP instead of improvising access.

Procedure

Standard new hire device setup sequence.

1
Review the requestConfirm the ticket is for a new hire setup and capture start date, department, and any platform notes.
2
Validate user informationConfirm full name, NetID, department, and role details if those affect access or software.
3
Confirm device assignmentDetermine whether a new device is being prepared or an existing device is being assigned to the new hire.
4
Complete the imaging pathUse the Windows MDT SOP for Windows devices or the Jamf imaging SOP for Apple devices.
5
Configure access and appsConfirm account readiness, required groups, shared drives, and standard or department-specific applications.
6
Validate and prepare handoffTest login, network, apps, reporting tools, and confirm charger or accessories are ready for the user.

Validation and Closeout

Use these checks before notifying the new hire or manager.

LoginThe user account can access the device correctly.
ApplicationsStandard and required apps are installed.
AccessExpected permissions and groups are in place.
Security reportingKACE, Cortex XDR, InsightVM, and Jamf where relevant are reporting.
Asset recordsKACE and Salesforce (E360) reflect the assigned user.
Handoff readinessThe device and accessories are ready for delivery.

Escalation and Recovery

Escalate when ownership, imaging, or access cannot be resolved inside the standard onboarding path.

No assigned hardware or unclear ownershipCoordinate with inventory or escalate before proceeding.
Imaging or enrollment failsEscalate through the MDT or Jamf path rather than improvising a partial setup.
Required access is missing or unusualEscalate to Endpoint Management with role details and requested access scope.
