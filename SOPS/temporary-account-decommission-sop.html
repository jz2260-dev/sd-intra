SOP - Internal Procedure

Temporary Account Decommission Review

Use this SOP to review temporary accounts, confirm sponsor intent, and route either extension requests or deprovisioning actions through the correct teams.

Owner
Stern IT Service Desk
Last reviewed
August 4, 2026
Overview Prerequisites Decision Points Procedure Validation Escalation

SOP Flow

Identify the account, find the original sponsor and end date, request confirmation, then route the outcome into either extension or deprovisioning.

Overview

Use this SOP for annual or periodic review of temporary account lifecycle and sponsor confirmation.

Primary outcomeTemporary accounts are either extended through the proper approval path or submitted for deprovisioning with documented sponsor confirmation.
Use whenA temporary account reaches review time or its proposed end date.
Do not use whenYou need to directly disable the account yourself or when sponsor ownership is still unresolved.

Prerequisites

Confirm these before contacting the sponsor.

Account type and NetID are identified.

Original request source can be reviewed in ServiceNow, legacy records, or HATS notes.

Sponsor name and proposed end date are available or traceable.

Asana and ticket record are open for updates.

Decision Points

Use these checks before routing the outcome.

Can the sponsor be confirmed?If not, stop and resolve ownership before taking action on the account.
Did the sponsor request an extension?If yes, forward to the Accounts team and enforce the one-year maximum rule.
Did the sponsor confirm deprovisioning?If yes, route the request for decommissioning and document the approval trail clearly.

Procedure

Standard temporary account review and decommission routing sequence.

1
Identify the accountConfirm the account is a temporary worker, fellow, scholar, or equivalent temporary identity.
2
Locate the original requestUse current or legacy ServiceNow records and HATS notes to capture sponsor and proposed end date. Contact Johnny if older system access is needed.
3
Contact the sponsorSend the review email asking whether the account is still needed, including account name, NetID, proposed end date, and any known notes.
4
Process the responseIf the sponsor wants an extension, forward the request to accounts@stern.nyu.edu. If the sponsor confirms removal, route the deprovisioning request and notify the relevant owner.
5
Update the systems of recordUpdate Asana status, comments, proposed end date or deprovision note, and copy the sponsor response into the ticket.
6
Send the follow-up confirmationNotify the sponsor whether the account was forwarded for extension review or for deprovisioning.

Validation and Closeout

Use these checks before completing the review task.

Sponsor confirmationA sponsor response or verified escalation path exists.
OutcomeThe account is routed for extension or deprovisioning.
Extension ruleAny extension request stays within the one-year maximum per request.
AsanaStatus, dates, and comments are updated.
Ticket notesResponse and actions taken are documented.

Escalation and Recovery

Escalate when sponsorship or lifecycle authority is unclear.

Sponsor cannot be foundEscalate after checking ServiceNow, HATS notes, and known legacy sources.
No sponsor response after follow-upEscalate with account details and outreach history.
Conflicting ownership or end-date informationPause action and confirm with the department before routing extension or removal.
