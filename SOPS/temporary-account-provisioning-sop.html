SOP - Internal Procedure

Temporary Account Provisioning

Use this SOP to validate approvals, create scoped temporary access, define expiration, and document the account clearly for later review and cleanup.

Owner
Stern IT Service Desk
Last reviewed
August 4, 2026
Overview Prerequisites Decision Points Procedure Validation Escalation

SOP Flow

Verify justification and approvals first, define the smallest viable access scope, create the account with an expiration, then document and schedule cleanup.

Overview

Use this SOP when creating temporary Stern or NYU-related work accounts.

Primary outcomeA temporary account is created with approved scope, explicit expiration, and auditable documentation.
Use whenThe request is for a visiting scholar, contractor, student worker, teaching fellow, or similar temporary access need.
Do not use whenThe request lacks approval, business justification, or a defined end date.

Prerequisites

Confirm these before account creation begins.

Request includes business justification and sponsoring context.

Manager or department approval is documented.

User details and required access scope are complete.

Start date and end date are defined and do not exceed one year.

Decision Points

Use these checks before granting access.

Is approval complete?If not, do not proceed. Request the approval path in the ticket first.
Is the requested access minimal and specific?If not, reduce scope before provisioning rather than granting broad permissions.
Does the duration exceed one year?If yes, stop and request a corrected duration or a reviewed extension path.

Procedure

Standard temporary account creation sequence.

1
Review the requestConfirm the ticket is for a temporary account and includes business justification.
2
Verify approvalsConfirm manager or department approval is present. If missing, stop and request it in the ticket.
3
Validate scope and durationConfirm user details, required systems, start date, and end date, ensuring the initial duration does not exceed one year.
4
Create the account in Active DirectoryCreate the account using the correct naming convention, set a temporary password, define expiration if supported, and add required groups.
5
Grant only required accessAssign the minimum necessary groups, applications, and device access without over-permissioning.
6
Communicate and documentProvide credentials through an approved secure method, instruct the user appropriately, and record username, expiration, approvals, and granted access in the ticket.

Validation and Closeout

Use these checks before completing the request.

ApprovalApproval is documented.
Account creationThe temporary account exists and is usable.
Access scopeOnly required permissions were granted.
ExpirationA defined end date is in place.
CommunicationUser or sponsor was notified through an approved method.
DocumentationTicket contains username, access scope, expiration, and approval notes.

Escalation and Recovery

Escalate when the request introduces risk or falls outside standard provisioning rules.

Approval or identity is uncertainStop and escalate rather than creating the account on incomplete evidence.
Requested access is unusually broad or sensitiveEscalate to Endpoint Management or the appropriate approver for review.
Account creation or group assignment failsEscalate with request details, attempted steps, and the failure point.
