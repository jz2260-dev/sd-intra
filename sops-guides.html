Core SOPs

Windows desktop imaging (MDT)Deploy, validate, update, and hand off Stern desktop builds. Windows laptop imaging (MDT)Build laptops, create local users, validate updates, and close out assets. Windows imaging fallback (KACE)Use the KACE path for unsupported or non-standard Windows hardware. Apple imaging and enrollment (Jamf)Enroll Apple devices through Jamf and validate apps, security tools, and ownership. New hire device setupPrepare onboarding devices, validate access, and complete handoff readiness. Device intake and request handlingValidate intake source, approvals, data responsibility, and downstream routing. Ethernet and network accessUpdate VLAN and DNS records, validate wired connectivity, and close out network requests. Classroom podium PC loginRestore podium access quickly and confirm presentation readiness for class. Temporary account provisioningCreate temporary access with approvals, expiration, and audit-ready documentation. Temporary account decommission reviewConfirm sponsor intent and route extension or deprovisioning actions.
